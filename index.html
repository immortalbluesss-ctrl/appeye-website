Questions

Common questions

How many apps and websites can I manage?+
Starter: 1 app + 1 website. Pro: 5 apps + 5 websites. Enterprise: 25 apps + 25 websites. Each gets its own revenue tracking, analytics, SEO monitoring and ad optimization in one unified dashboard.
How does web page sales tracking work?+
You paste one tracking line into your web page — like Google Analytics. APPEYE then monitors every visitor, sale, lead and conversion in real time. No coding knowledge required.
How does the 14-day free trial work?+
Sign up with your email and phone number — no credit card required. You get full access for 14 days. At the end, choose to subscribe or your account pauses. Your data is always safe.
Can I pay in Indian Rupees?+
Yes. Indian customers pay in INR via Razorpay — UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking all accepted. International customers pay in USD or CAD via Stripe.
Is there a refund policy?+
APPEYE has a strict no-refund policy. All payments are final once your 14-day trial ends. This is why we offer a generous free trial — so you can test everything before committing. Full policy at appeye.app/terms.html
Can I white-label APPEYE for clients?+
Yes — the Enterprise plan includes white-labeling. Rebrand APPEYE with your agency name and logo and sell it to clients as your own product.
Do I need to know coding?+
No coding required for apps. For web page tracking, you paste one line — like adding Google Analytics. If you built a website, you can do this.
